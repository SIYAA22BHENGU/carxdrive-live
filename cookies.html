Cookie Policy

This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies may be used when you visit carXdrive.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files or identifiers that can be stored on a device when a website is visited. Similar technologies may also be used to remember preferences, measure activity, support website functionality and help provide advertising.

How carXdrive may use cookies

carXdrive may use cookies or similar technologies for website functionality, security, saved preferences, analytics, traffic measurement and advertising.

Some technologies may be provided by third-party services used by carXdrive, including Google Analytics and Google AdSense.

Essential and preference storage

Some browser storage may be used to support website features or remember choices. For example, carXdrive may store a visitor's cookie banner choice so the same notice does not need to be shown on every page visit.

Blocking certain storage through your browser may affect how some website features behave.

Google Analytics

carXdrive uses Google Analytics to understand website traffic and how visitors interact with pages and features.

Google Analytics may process information such as pages visited, approximate location, device and browser information, referral source and interactions with the website.

This information helps carXdrive understand which pages are useful, identify technical issues and improve the website.

Google AdSense and advertising cookies

carXdrive uses or may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, pixels, IP addresses and similar technologies to serve ads, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and understand ad interactions.

Depending on a visitor's location, settings and consent choices, advertisements may be personalised or non-personalised.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on visits to carXdrive or other websites.

Visitors can manage personalised advertising through Google's Ads Settings .

Consent and cookie choices

carXdrive provides a cookie notice that allows visitors to record an Accept or Reject choice. The website may store that choice in the browser so it can be remembered on later visits.

Cookie and consent requirements can vary by country or region. Additional consent controls may be used where required by applicable law or by the services used on carXdrive.

Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers allow you to view, delete or block cookies and site storage. You can usually find these controls in the browser's privacy, security or site-settings menu.

Blocking cookies may affect website functionality and may also change how analytics or advertising services operate.

Third-party services

carXdrive does not directly control cookies or similar technologies placed by third-party providers. Their use of information is governed by their own privacy and cookie policies.

More information about Google's use of information from websites and apps that use its services is available through Google's information for partner websites .

This policy may be updated when carXdrive changes its website features, analytics tools, advertising providers or legal requirements.

Our data approach

Clear about where the numbers come from.

carXdrive combines structured vehicle specifications with available manufacturer information and published road-test data to make research easier. We do not claim that every performance figure shown on the platform was measured directly by carXdrive.

S

Specifications

Vehicle specifications can vary by market, model year, trim and optional equipment. The exact variant should always be checked before using a figure for a purchase decision.

P

Published results

Performance and test figures may come from manufacturer claims, published road tests or compiled reference estimates, depending on what data is available for a vehicle.

C

Context matters

Acceleration, braking, handling and efficiency results can change with tyres, temperature, road surface, altitude, vehicle load and testing method. Small differences should be interpreted carefully.

Using the database

Research the exact car, not just the model name.

A single model can be sold with different engines, transmissions, drivetrains, trims and equipment packages. Those differences can change performance, fuel use, weight, safety equipment and even the way a vehicle feels on the road.

carXdrive separates vehicles by model year and variant where possible so you can research the specification that is actually relevant to your comparison. Use the search and brand filters above to find a vehicle, then open its full profile for the available data.

Research tip
Always check the exact year, engine and variant before comparing two cars.

What you can research

More than basic specifications.

Vehicle profiles can include information across performance, engineering, practicality, safety and technology.

01

Engine and performance

Review engine type, power, torque, transmission, drivetrain, acceleration and top speed. Where available, additional rolling acceleration and independent test figures provide more context.

02

Chassis, brakes and tyres

Compare kerb weight, suspension layout, steering, tyres, wheels, braking hardware and available handling data. These details help explain why cars with similar power figures can behave differently.

03

Practicality and efficiency

Seat count, boot capacity, dimensions, fuel-tank size and combined consumption can be just as important as performance when choosing a daily vehicle or family car.

04

Safety and technology

Explore available safety equipment, driver-assistance systems, infotainment, smartphone connectivity, displays and convenience features to understand how well-equipped a vehicle is.

Reading vehicle data

Why specifications can differ between sources.

M

Market differences

Equipment, engine output and trim levels can vary by country. A car sold under the same name in two markets may not have an identical specification.

Y

Model-year changes

Manufacturers can update software, engines, safety equipment, transmissions or option packages during a model's production run. Always check the model year shown on the profile.

T

Test conditions

Acceleration, braking and fuel results can change with weather, tyres, road surface, altitude, vehicle load and testing method. Published results should be treated as research data, not guarantees.

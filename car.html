Understanding the data

How to use carXdrive vehicle pages

https://omg10.com/4/11661967

Each vehicle page brings together structured specifications, published performance information and research notes for a specific model, model year and variant where that information is available.

Check the exact variant

Engines, transmissions, drivetrains, equipment and performance can differ significantly between trims that share the same model name. Always compare the exact variant shown on the page.

Performance figures can vary

Acceleration, braking, fuel economy and other performance results depend on testing conditions, tyres, weather, vehicle load, surface, driver technique and measurement method.

Published and reference data

Depending on the vehicle, carXdrive may use manufacturer specifications, published road-test information or compiled reference estimates. carXdrive does not claim that every listed figure was measured directly by carXdrive.

Use more than one metric

Horsepower and acceleration are only part of the picture. Weight, braking, tyres, fuel economy, practicality, safety equipment and drivetrain layout can all affect how suitable a vehicle is for a particular buyer.
